# Treasure Island Readers ⛵

A play-based reading game for two young readers: **Little Captain** (pre-reader, around age 4) and **Big Captain** (early reader, around age 7).
The aim is to teach *why* reading matters as well as *how* to read: in the "Reading Quest" missions, the child can only get the treasure, stay safe, make the pancakes or deliver the parcel by reading.

## Play it
Open `index.html` in Safari, Chrome or Edge. It needs no install, no internet and no accounts. The game tries to go full screen when you tap Start (and there is a ⛶ button on the map and in every game). On an iPhone or in an embedded page, full screen is not available: use Share → **Add to Home Screen** instead.
Turn the volume up: every prompt is spoken aloud.

## Challenge Island
- 🔒 **Captain's Challenge is locked** until the child has finished every other island on their track at least once. The locked island shows progress ("3 of 6"), the unfinished islands get a ✨ marker, and tapping it gently says how many are left. It unlocks with a celebration the next time the map is shown, and stays open.
- After the quiz, the **"Islands to practice" are buttons**. Tap one and the treasure screen offers **⛵ Sail to that island**. Recommended islands wear a pulsing **"Practice me!"** flag on the map until their next finished voyage. Only islands on the child's own track are recommended.

## Points
- 🪙 **Coins:** 3 for a correct answer on the first try, 1 for getting there later, never taken away. Coins are spent in the Harbor Shop on boat decorations.
- ⭐ **Stars and stickers** for every finished voyage, collected in the sticker book.
- **Ranks:** Deckhand → Sailor → First Mate → Captain → Admiral.
- 🦸 **Reading Superpowers** earned in Reading Quest are listed on the "Why Read?" scroll on the map.

## Grown-ups panel
Hold the ⚙️ gear for 3 seconds to open it. From there you can:
- rename the kids and change their avatars and tracks;
- set the speech speed, pick a voice (English voices, best first, with a ▶ test button) and switch the **expressive voice** on or off;
- choose whether the game opens in full screen on start;
- unlock the Challenge Island early for one child;
- see accuracy and level for each skill;
- see quiz history;
- reset progress.

Progress is saved in the browser on that device only.

## Voice
Speech is expressive: the game picks the most natural English voice it can find and varies pitch, speed and phrasing (excited praise, gentle "Try again", rising questions). For the best sound on an iPad, download an Enhanced or Premium voice in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.

## Tip
The game sets up the "why"; you make it stick. When reading pays off in real life (a dock sign, a recipe, a game menu), say so out loud.
